Report a mistake

If a lesson, an example or a quiz answer is wrong, please tell us. It helps to include:

  • the address of the page,
  • the sentence or question that is wrong,
  • what you think it should say.

What we cannot do

  • We do not offer private lessons or correct personal writing.
  • We do not issue certificates or official level results.
  • We cannot help with accounts on other websites. This site is not connected to englishlearner.com.

Privacy and ads

For questions about data or advertising, read the privacy policy first. You can also write to the email address above.
