The rules in short

  • at: a clock time or a point. at 3 o'clock, at noon, at the door, at the bus stop
  • on: a day, a date or a surface. on Monday, on 15 March, on the table, on the wall
  • in: a longer period or a space with limits. in May, in 1995, in the morning, in London, in the kitchen
  • by: not later than a time. by Friday, by 6 o'clock
  • with: the tool you use, or the person who is with you. with a pen, with her friends
