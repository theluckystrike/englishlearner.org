Check your writing and pronunciation

These tools run in your browser. The checkers use rules and can miss mistakes, so treat each result as a hint.

  • Sentence analyzer

    Paste a sentence. See the subject, the main verb, its tense and voice, the clauses and the role of each word. Automatic, so check unusual sentences.

    Experimental

  • Verb tense checker

    Name each tense in your text and catch common mistakes such as she don’t, I have went or I live here since 2020.

    Experimental

  • Article checker

    Check a and an by sound, and find a missing or extra the in common patterns.

    Experimental

  • Pronunciation guide

    IPA and stress for over 9,000 common words, with listening in American or British voices when your device has them.

    9,000+ words

  • IELTS and TOEFL comparison

    Compare IELTS, TOEFL and CEFR levels with the official tables, and try a short practice set.

    Official tables

Practice in your browser
