English practice tools

Six free tools for self-study. They run in your browser, mark your answers at once and need no account.

  • English level test

    30 grammar and reading questions, five for each CEFR level from A1 to C2. You get a rough level and a score for each level.

  • Grammar exercises

    Multiple-choice questions on verb tenses, articles, prepositions and irregular verbs at three levels. Every answer comes with a short explanation.

  • Irregular verbs list and quiz

    A searchable table of common irregular verbs with base form, past simple and past participle. Then a quiz on the past simple.

  • Vocabulary quiz

    40 useful adjectives from A2 to C1. Match each word to its meaning, or each meaning to its word.

  • Idioms dictionary

    More than 120 common English idioms. Each has a plain meaning, an example sentence and a note on how formal it is.

  • Preposition practice

    Ten fill-the-gap questions on in, on, at, by and with. Each answer explains the rule.

Read the rule first

A quiz tells you what you got wrong. A lesson tells you why. If a tool shows a weak point, read the lesson on it.
