Common pronunciation rules

Silent ‘e’ – hope, make, late (vowel becomes long)
Silent ‘k’ – knife, know, knee (k before n)
Silent ‘b’ – comb, thumb, debt (mb, bt)
‘sh’ /ʃ/ – ship, fish, action
‘th’ /θ/ & /ð/ – think, this, mother
‘ng’ /ŋ/ – sing, long, walking
short /ɪ/ vs /iː/ – ship vs sheep
‘ch’ /tʃ/ – chip, teacher, catch
