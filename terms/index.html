Terms of use

Last updated 17 November 2026

These terms cover the free lessons, quizzes and practice tools on englishlearner.org. By using the site you agree to them. They are short and written in plain English.

Who runs English Learner

English Learner is an independent site run by Michael Lip, a developer in Poland. You can contact him at support@zovo.one. The About page explains how lessons are written and checked.

Free use

All lessons, quizzes and practice tools are free. You need no account. You may use them for your own study. Teachers may share a link to any page with a class and print a page for classroom use.

Do not copy whole lessons, quizzes or tool code to another website or app. Short quotes with a link back are fine. Do not use bots or scripts that load pages at a high rate, and do not try to break or overload the site.

Learning help, not an official result

The level test, the score estimators and the checker tools give an estimate to guide your study. They are not official CEFR, IELTS, TOEFL or Cambridge results, and the site does not issue certificates. Only the exam boards can give an official score.

The checker tools look for common learner mistakes. They can miss a mistake or flag a correct sentence. When a tool is not sure, it says so.

Mistakes and changes

Each lesson is reviewed before it goes live, but a page can still contain a mistake. If you find one, write to us with the page address and the sentence that is wrong. We fix confirmed errors. We may change, add or remove lessons and tools at any time.

Ads, analytics and links

Free pages may show ads served by Google AdSense, and we use Google Analytics to count visits. The privacy policy explains what is sent, the consent choice and how to change it. Links to other sites are for your information. We do not control those sites.

Paid workbook

The workbook is a separate paid download. The checkout page shows the price before you pay. The workbook page states its own purchase and refund terms, and those apply to the purchase. These terms apply to the free site.

Liability

The site is provided as it is, without a promise that it is always available or free of errors. To the extent the law allows, we are not liable for loss that comes from using the site. Nothing in these terms limits rights that consumer law gives you and that cannot be excluded.

Questions

Write to support@zovo.one and put "englishlearner.org" in the subject line. The Contact page lists what to include.
