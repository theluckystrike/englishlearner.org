Articles in English

A2 to B1 About 10 minutes

The key idea

Use a before a consonant sound. Use an before a vowel sound.

Read the rulesTry the quiz

English has two kinds of article. "A" and "an" are indefinite. "The" is definite. Sometimes the correct choice is no article at all. This lesson gives you the rules in order, from the easiest to the hardest.

By Michael Lip

A or an? Listen to the sound

Use a before a consonant sound. Use an before a vowel sound. The rule is about sound, not spelling.

A or an? Listen to the sound
ExampleWhy
a book, a car, a houseThe next word starts with a consonant sound.
an apple, an egg, an umbrellaThe next word starts with a vowel sound.
an hour, an honest manThe "h" is silent, so the word starts with a vowel sound.
a university, a European city, a one-way streetThese words start with a vowel letter but a consonant sound: "you" and "w".
an MBA, an FBI agent, a UN reportSay the letters. "M" sounds like "em". "U" sounds like "you".

When to use a or an

Use "a" or "an" only with a singular countable noun.

  • The first time you mention something. I saw a dog in the park.
  • One of many. Can I borrow a pen? Any pen is fine.
  • Jobs. She is a doctor. He works as an engineer.
  • "Per" in rates. twice a week, 90 kilometres an hour

Do not use "a" or "an" with plural nouns or with uncountable nouns. Say some information, not Incorrect example. an information. See the lesson on countable and uncountable nouns.

When to use the

Use "the" when the listener knows which thing you mean.

  • The second mention. I saw a dog in the park. The dog was chasing a ball.
  • There is only one. the sun, the moon, the internet, the sky
  • The situation makes it clear. Can you close the door? Both people know which door.
  • Superlatives and ordinals. the best film, the first day, the last page
  • A phrase after the noun makes it specific. the capital of France, the man who called yesterday
  • Musical instruments, usually. She plays the piano. American English often leaves "the" out here.

When to use no article

  • Plural and uncountable nouns in general statements. Dogs are loyal. Water boils at 100 degrees Celsius at sea level.
  • Languages, school subjects and sports. I speak Spanish. She studies history. They play tennis.
  • Meals. We had lunch at one.
  • Some fixed phrases about places. go to school, go to work, go to bed, go home, be at home
  • Transport with "by". by bus, by train, by car

Compare the general and the specific meaning:

  • I like music. Music in general.
  • I like the music in this film. One specific set of music.

No articleWith "the"

Common mistakes

Incorrect

She is teacher.

Correct

She is a teacher.

Use a before a singular job noun with a consonant sound.

Incorrect

I waited for a hour.

Correct

I waited for an hour.

Hour starts with a vowel sound.

Incorrect

He studies at an university.

Correct

He studies at a university.

University starts with a consonant sound.

Incorrect

The life is short.

Correct

Life is short.

Use no article for life in general.

Incorrect

I need an advice.

Correct

I need some advice.

Advice is uncountable.

Incorrect

We went to the bed late.

Correct

We went to bed late.

Go to bed is a fixed phrase.

Incorrect

It is best film of the year.

Correct

It is the best film of the year.

Use the with a superlative here.
