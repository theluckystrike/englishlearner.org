Articles in English: a, an, the

English has two kinds of article. "A" and "an" are indefinite. "The" is definite. Sometimes the correct choice is no article at all. This lesson gives you the rules in order, from the easiest to the hardest.

Level: A2 to B1. Reading time: about 10 minutes.

1. A or an? Listen to the sound

Use a before a consonant sound. Use an before a vowel sound. The rule is about sound, not spelling.

ExampleWhy
a book, a car, a houseThe next word starts with a consonant sound.
an apple, an egg, an umbrellaThe next word starts with a vowel sound.
an hour, an honest manThe "h" is silent, so the word starts with a vowel sound.
a university, a European city, a one-way streetThese words start with a vowel letter but a consonant sound: "you" and "w".
an MBA, an FBI agent, a UN reportSay the letters. "M" sounds like "em". "U" sounds like "you".

2. When to use a or an

Use "a" or "an" only with a singular countable noun.

  • The first time you mention something. I saw a dog in the park.
  • One of many. Can I borrow a pen? Any pen is fine.
  • Jobs. She is a doctor. He works as an engineer.
  • "Per" in rates. twice a week, 90 kilometres an hour

Do not use "a" or "an" with plural nouns or with uncountable nouns. Say some information, not an information. See the lesson on countable and uncountable nouns.

3. When to use the

Use "the" when the listener knows which thing you mean.

  • The second mention. I saw a dog in the park. The dog was chasing a ball.
  • There is only one. the sun, the moon, the internet, the sky
  • The situation makes it clear. Can you close the door? Both people know which door.
  • Superlatives and ordinals. the best film, the first day, the last page
  • A phrase after the noun makes it specific. the capital of France, the man who called yesterday
  • Musical instruments, usually. She plays the piano. American English often leaves "the" out here.

4. When to use no article

  • Plural and uncountable nouns in general statements. Dogs are loyal. Water boils at 100 degrees Celsius at sea level.
  • Languages, school subjects and sports. I speak Spanish. She studies history. They play tennis.
  • Meals. We had lunch at one.
  • Some fixed phrases about places. go to school, go to work, go to bed, go home, be at home
  • Transport with "by". by bus, by train, by car

Compare the general and the specific meaning:

  • I like music. Music in general.
  • I like the music in this film. One specific set of music.

5. Articles with place names

No articleWith "the"
Most countries and cities: France, Japan, CairoPlural names and names with "kingdom", "states" or "republic": the Netherlands, the Philippines, the United Kingdom, the United States, the Czech Republic
Continents: Asia, AfricaOceans, seas and rivers: the Pacific, the Mediterranean, the Nile, the Thames
Single mountains and lakes: Mount Everest, Lake GenevaMountain ranges and island groups: the Alps, the Andes, the Canary Islands
Most streets and squares: Oxford Street, Times SquareDeserts: the Sahara, the Gobi

6. Common mistakes

WrongRight
She is teacher.She is a teacher.
I waited for a hour.I waited for an hour.
He studies at an university.He studies at a university.
The life is short.Life is short.
I need an advice.I need some advice.
We went to the bed late.We went to bed late.
It is best film of the year.It is the best film of the year.

Quiz: a, an, the or no article?

Choose a, an, the, or nothing for each gap.

  1. My sister is ___ engineer.
  2. We stayed at ___ hotel near the station. ___ hotel was very quiet.
  3. It takes about ___ hour to get there.
  4. ___ Nile is longer than ___ Thames.
  5. I usually have ___ breakfast at seven.
  6. She wants to study at ___ university in Canada.
  7. ___ honesty is important in a friendship.
  8. This is ___ most interesting book I have read this year.
  9. He goes to work by ___ train.
  10. They live in ___ Netherlands.
Show the answers
  1. an. A job, and "engineer" starts with a vowel sound.
  2. a, then The. First mention, then second mention.
  3. an. The "h" in "hour" is silent.
  4. The, the. Rivers take "the".
  5. no article. Meals take no article.
  6. a. "University" starts with a "you" sound.
  7. No article. An uncountable noun in a general statement.
  8. the. A superlative.
  9. no article. "By" plus transport takes no article.
  10. the. A plural country name.
