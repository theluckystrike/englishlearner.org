English grammar lessons

Choose a topic and practise its rules.

Take the level test

Each grammar lesson ends with a short quiz with answers. Lessons note where British and American English differ.

Start with these lessons

  • In, on and at

    Time: at 7 o'clock, on Monday, in May. Place: at the door, on the table, in the kitchen. Plus when to use no preposition.

    A1 to A2

  • Articles in English

    A or an by sound. First and second mention. When to use no article. Articles with place names.

    A2 to B1

  • Countable and uncountable nouns

    Much and many, few and little, some and any. A list of nouns that have no plural in English.

    A2 to B1

Build on the basics

  • Present perfect or past simple

    Finished time or a link to now. For and since. Just, already and yet. Been or gone.

    B1

  • English conditionals

    The four "if" patterns, mixed conditionals, unless, and where the comma goes.

    B1 to B2

  • Past perfect tense

    Had + past participle for the earlier of two past events. By the time, already, reported speech and regrets.

    B1 to B2

  • Modal verbs

    Can, could, may, might, must, should and have to. Ability, permission, advice, rules and how sure you are.

    A2 to B2

  • Gerunds and infinitives

    Enjoy doing or want to do. Verbs that change meaning, like stop, remember and try. A searchable verb list.

    B1 to B2

  • Passive voice

    Be + past participle in every tense. When to use it, when to add by, and how to change active to passive.

    B1 to B2
