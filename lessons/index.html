English grammar lessons

Each lesson explains one grammar point. It gives the rules, correct examples, the common mistakes and a ten-question quiz with answers. Lessons note where British and American English differ.

Beginner and elementary (A1 to A2)

  • Prepositions: in, on, at

    Time: at 7 o'clock, on Monday, in May. Place: at the door, on the table, in the kitchen. Plus when to use no preposition.

  • Articles: a, an, the

    A or an by sound. First and second mention. When to use no article. Articles with place names.

  • Countable and uncountable nouns

    Much and many, few and little, some and any. A list of nouns that have no plural in English.

Intermediate (B1 to B2)

  • Present perfect or past simple?

    Finished time or a link to now. For and since. Just, already and yet. Been or gone.

  • Conditionals: zero, first, second, third

    The four "if" patterns, mixed conditionals, unless, and where the comma goes.

Guides

  • CEFR levels A1 to C2

    What each level means in plain words and what to study at each one.
