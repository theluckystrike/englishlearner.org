About English Learner

English Learner is a free self-study site for people who are learning English as a second language. It has short grammar lessons and practice tools.

Who runs this site

The site is built and maintained by Michael Lip, an independent software developer. His other projects are listed at zovo.one.

English Learner is not a school. It does not employ teachers, it does not give certificates and it is not an exam board.

This site is englishlearner.org. It is not connected to englishlearner.com or to any other site with a similar name.

What you will find here

  • Grammar lessons. Each lesson covers one point with rules, examples, common mistakes and a quiz.
  • Practice tools. Quizzes and reference lists that run in your browser.
  • A level test that gives a rough CEFR level.

How the content is made

Lessons and quiz questions are written with the help of AI tools. They cover standard, widely taught English grammar. Where British and American English differ, the lesson says so.

We try hard to make every rule and every example correct. Mistakes are still possible. If you find one, please tell us so that it can be corrected.

The level test and the quizzes are for practice. Their results are a guide only. They are not official exam scores.

How the site is paid for

The site is free to use. It is paid for by advertising from Google AdSense. The privacy policy explains how the ads work and how to turn them off.

The footer links to BeLikeNative, a writing assistant made by the same developer. That link is a promotion of our own product.
