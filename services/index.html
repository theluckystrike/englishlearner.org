Custom English tools for your class or school

Everything on this site stays free. If you teach English and want a tool built for your own students, I can build it. You get a fixed price by email before any work starts.

I'm Michael Lip, the developer behind englishlearner.org. I've shipped 108 Chrome extensions with over 150,000 users. My code is public on GitHub. I'm a developer, not an English teacher, so you bring the teaching content and I build the tool around it.

What I can build

  • Your own level test

    A placement test like the one on this site, with your questions, your levels and your school name. It runs on a link or inside your website.

    From 149 USD, one time

  • A quiz set from your word list

    Send a vocabulary list or a grammar unit. You get a set of self-marking quizzes your students can open on a phone, with no sign-up.

    From 99 USD, one time

  • The level test on your site

    The free englishlearner.org level test, without our ads, embedded on your school or tutoring site. It sends you each result by email.

    From 199 USD, one time

    A daily vocabulary trainer as a web app or a Telegram bot, built around your course book or syllabus.

    From 399 USD, one time

  • Writing feedback with AI

    A tool that checks your students' short essays against your rubric and explains each mistake in simple English. You review the setup before students use it.

How it works

  1. Email support@zovo.one with what you teach, how many students you have and what the tool should do.
  2. I reply in writing with a fixed price and a delivery date. Most small tools take one to two weeks.
