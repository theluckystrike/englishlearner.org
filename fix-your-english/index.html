Three mistakes from Part 1

Three examples in the same format as the workbook. The free sample shows real pages.

  • Verbs

    Wrong
    Incorrect: I am agree with you.
    Right
    I agree with you.
    Rule
    "Agree" is a verb. Do not put am, is or are before it.
    中文说明
    很多人把 agree 当成 happy 那样的形容词，所以在前面加了 am、is 或 are。agree 本身就是动词，意思是“同意”，直接说 I agree。
    Try it. Correct this sentence: She is agree with the plan.

    She agrees with the plan.

  • Countable and uncountable nouns

    Wrong
    Incorrect: He has many homeworks.
    Right
    He has a lot of homework.
    Rule
    "Homework" is uncountable. It has no plural, and you do not use "many" with it. Use "a lot of".
    中文说明
    中文可以说“很多作业”“两份作业”，但英语的 homework 是不可数名词，没有复数，不能加 -s，也不能和 many 连用。可以说 a lot of homework。
    Try it. Correct this sentence: We need more informations.

    We need more information.

  • Linking words

    Wrong
    Incorrect: Although it rained, but we went out.
    Right
    Although it rained, we went out.
    Rule
    "Although" and "but" both show a contrast. Use only one of them in a sentence.
    中文说明
    中文“虽然……但是……”常常成对使用，但英语里 although 和 but 只能选一个：Although it rained, we went out. 或 It rained, but we went out.
    Try it. Correct this sentence: Although he was tired, but he finished the report.

    Although he was tired, he finished the report. Or: He was tired, but he finished the report.
